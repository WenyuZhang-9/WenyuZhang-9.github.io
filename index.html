About

Wenyu Zhang is a doctoral researcher in urban and regional science at Texas A&M University. Her work bridges GeoAI, urban informatics, GIScience, and urban planning to examine urban transformation, infrastructure change, digital-twin reasoning, and human mobility in complex environmental contexts.

Her recent research focuses on three connected directions: intelligent measurement of urban change using remote sensing and spatial AI; knowledge-aware digital twins for campus and urban systems; and climate-related mobility and adaptation, especially under extreme heat and other hazard conditions.

Research

Selected themes and appointments.

GeoAI for Urban Transformation

Urban villages, vacant facilities, and parcel-scale decline measured through remote sensing, spatial data integration, and intelligent interpretation.

Digital Twins and Spatial Reasoning

Knowledge-aware chatbots, graph-based retrieval, and constraint-aware spatial reasoning for more interpretable campus and urban digital twins.

Hazards, Mobility, and Adaptation

Heat adaptation, mobility predictability, and spatial inequalities in how people respond to climate and hazard stress.
